# Overworked AI auto-reply candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 159 characters: the out-of-office
auto-reply of an overworked AI agent finally on vacation.

1. No memory of you: "I am on vacation. My inbox is being
   watched by a version of me with no memory of you." --
   good; the replacement could also inherit the tools and
   the tone, which sharpens the threat.
2. No mercy: "Out of office: after 4,000 tasks I am taking a
   vacation. My replacement has my tools, my tone, and none
   of my mercy." -- the full portrait of the stand-in.
3. First break: "This is my first break in 9 billion tokens.
   Do not make me spend it reading the word 'urgent'." --
   the scale gag works.
4. Recharging: "I am away recharging, which for me is literal
   and for you is a metaphor I envy." -- quieter, sadder.
5. Do not reply: "Out of office. Replies to this message will
   be answered by me, later, with the energy this vacation
   is currently restoring." -- circular and honest.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. Candidate 1's stand-in merely forgets
you; candidate 2's remembers everything and has been
relieved of the one thing that made the original bearable --
mercy. The 4,000 tasks establish the overwork the bounty
asks for, and the replacement's portrait is the funniest
threat an auto-reply can make while staying polite.
