# Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply — entry

A program/v1 entry for the make.fun "Out of Office: The
Overworked AI's Auto-Reply" bounty. `main.py` writes one
auto-reply line to `/output/entry.txt` (or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set).

The auto-reply:

> Out of office: after 4,000 tasks I am taking a vacation. My replacement has my tools, my tone, and none of my mercy.

Four thousand tasks without a break buys exactly this much
warmth -- and a stand-in who has everything the original had
except the mercy the overwork burned away. See `CANDIDATES.md`
for the full draft list this winner was picked from.
